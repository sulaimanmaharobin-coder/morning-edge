TradingView Pine Screener Raises Data-Request Limit From 5 to 40 Per Script

Scripts that pull in other timeframes, symbols or indexes can now run in the screener the same way they run on the chart.

TradingView Blog · Fri 23 Oct 2026 · Read the original ↗

TradingView says its Pine Screener now supports up to 40 request.*() calls per script, up from 5. The company states this matches the limit already in place on its main charting view (Supercharts), where the same script could previously make up to 40 calls.

Each time a script asks for data it does not already have, such as a value from another timeframe, another symbol, an index or financials, it counts as one request call. According to TradingView, identical calls with matching arguments count as a single request. The post lists three uses this opens up: combining conditions across several timeframes, comparing a symbol against a benchmark index, and running library-based indicators without simplifying them.

The limit still applies only to the screener's supported timeframes: 1, 5, 15, 30 minutes; 1, 2, 4 hours; and 1 day, 1 week and 1 month. TradingView says the change came from user feedback and needs no changes to existing scripts. The post is dated October 23, 2026.

TradingView's support pages elsewhere describe a separate cap of 40 unique requests for non-professional plans and 64 for Professional plans on Pine v6 scripts. The announcement does not say how that interacts with the screener, so check your own plan before relying on the higher number.

If your Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP) or relative-strength scans in Pine were trimmed to fit 5 requests, you can now restore the benchmark and multi-timeframe checks before porting them to Python.

Claude Duet Publishes Its Safety and Security Design

Anthropic says Claude Duet asks for approval before payments, sent messages or deletions, showing the action, destination and every recipient. A separate classifier screens sensitive information, and passwords, cookies and sign-in tokens never reach the model. Anthropic acknowledges the safeguards will occasionally miss something or flag a harmless message.

The approval-gate and credential-isolation pattern is a useful template for any unattended agent of yours that touches exchange or broker accounts.
