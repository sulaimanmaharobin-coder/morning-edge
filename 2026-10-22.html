Anthropic previews Claude Duet, an agent that keeps working while you are away

The research preview runs tasks in its own cloud browser and asks approval before sending, paying, signing or deleting.

Anthropic (Claude blog) · Wed 21 Oct 2026 · Read the original ↗

Anthropic introduced Claude Duet on October 21 as a proactive agent app. According to Anthropic, it brings you what needs attention, completes tasks you ask for, keeps working when you step away, and checks with you before it acts. It is a research preview behind a waitlist, starting with US Pro and Max subscribers, on iOS, Android and claude.ai.

Anthropic says Duet sends a morning briefing of important items from connected apps, then monitors through the day and notifies you only when something needs attention. It runs on a virtual machine with its own browser, so work continues when your devices are offline. Tasks range from scheduling meetings to building dashboards with dozens of tools.

The approval gate is the central design choice: before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, Duet shows exactly what it is about to do and waits for approval. Anthropic lists caveats: it is early, may misread threads or misjudge urgency, personal errands such as ticket booking are not fully developed, it shares usage limits with your regular Claude plan, and its browser cannot yet be displayed.

The launch drew attention on Hacker News, where the product page had 270 points and 218 comments about three hours after posting.

If you run Claude agents unattended overnight, Duet's approval-gate and background-VM design is a reference for how much autonomy to grant your own scanner and outreach bots, though it is US-only for now.

Omnara: open-source, model-agnostic alternative to Claude Managed Agents

The Apache-2.0 project saves every agent step in Postgres so agents resume after crashes, supports cron triggers with time zones, and runs on sandboxes from eleven providers or your own machines. Its README says it works with Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Bedrock and self-hosted models; it appeared on Hacker News as a Show HN with few votes so far.

Durable state plus cron scheduling is exactly what an unattended market-scanning agent team needs, and you could self-host it instead of relying on one vendor.
