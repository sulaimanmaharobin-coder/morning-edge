YC-backed Agent37 launches sandboxes for agents at under $5 a month

The Fall 2026 startup says it runs agent harnesses, including Claude Code, at a fraction of typical sandbox prices.

Hacker News (Launch HN) · Tue 13 Oct 2026 · Read the original ↗

Agent37, a Y Combinator Fall 2026 company, launched on Hacker News with low-cost cloud sandboxes built for running AI agents. The founder, Vishnu Kool, says the service costs under 10% of most sandbox providers.

Paid plans start at about $4.94 a month for a 2 vCPU, 4 GB configuration. The company says the average paid customer spends about $0.37 a month per sandbox. A free tier is available, which the company says costs it roughly $0.05 to $0.10 in infrastructure per user each month. It claims to be 2.8 times cheaper than Boat.dev and 94% cheaper than Fly.io Sprites. Those figures come from the company, not from independent tests.

The platform offers both gVisor-based containers and full VM sandboxes. It supports several agent harnesses, naming Hermes, OpenClaw and Claude Code. It also provides OpenAI-compatible API endpoints and managed integrations including Composio and BraveSearch. The launch thread had 77 points when checked and was ranked #10 on the front page.

If you run Claude Code teams unattended overnight, an isolated, cheap sandbox is a way to cap blast radius and cost, though the pricing claims need testing against your own workloads.
